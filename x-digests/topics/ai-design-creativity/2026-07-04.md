# AI Design Creativity
**Date:** 2026-07-04

## Key Examples
A creator shared a “badass” AI-generated music video for a Lemonheads cover, produced using @magnific for upscaling/enhancement and @RadTV__ for video generation. The result was highlighted as one of their best outputs, illustrating how specific tool chaining in the creative pipeline can elevate AI-generated music visuals from generic to polished and engaging. The post included direct links to the video, showing concrete before-and-after quality in a real music context.

A UI/UX designer joined the @Jggl_it platform and posted a video demo praising its blend of AI capabilities, creative tools, and social features. The designer expressed excitement for exploring future integrations, positioning the platform as a space where AI augments rather than replaces human design judgment in collaborative, community-driven environments.

Another example comes from an “MSC Online Creativity Masterclass” that teaches Canva + AI workflows for report and slide design. Participants learn free tools, tech skills, and AI integration for producing professional visual content, with the focus on practical application in educational and professional settings rather than pure generation.

## Insights
The conversation frames AI as a powerful partnership for creativity, where tools like Magnific and RadTV are used in targeted ways to solve specific production bottlenecks (e.g., video quality and music synchronization). This selective, workflow-specific adoption contrasts with blanket “use AI for everything” advice and shows how domain knowledge of both the creative medium and the AI tools leads to superior results.

Self-promotion around the intersection of AI, design, and storytelling for building premium digital identities suggests that practitioners are positioning themselves as curators of AI outputs rather than pure generators. The emphasis on human oversight appears in multiple threads, reinforcing that AI outputs require editing for voice, opinion, and authenticity—particularly when SEO or audience connection is the goal.

## Notable Opinions
One thread questioned why people “worship” certain AI tools in design contexts, pushing back against hype and highlighting the need for critical evaluation of utility versus novelty.
