# Content Marketing Experiments
**Date:** 2026-06-06

## Key Examples
@superframeworks published a detailed thread ranking content marketing agencies with a 2026 filter that treats strong GEO/AEO (Generative and Answer Engine Optimization) capabilities as table stakes. The ranking separates agencies into Done-for-you, Content-led growth, and Platform + creator networks categories, with realistic pricing bands from $5K/mo for basic DFY up to $25K+ for premium growth partners. The methodology explicitly excludes agencies that sell content services to avoid biased lists. https://x.com/i/status/2063150906195992598

A reply from @Wheatym5k11 on a Twitter growth tools list emphasized that schedulers do not meaningfully move small accounts. Instead, reply density on the original post within the first 30 minutes is the critical factor for early conversational momentum and algorithmic distribution. This observation came from direct experimentation with X automation and algo testing. https://x.com/Wheatym5k11/status/2063148200039444799

## Insights
Content marketing discussions in 2026 are shifting from "how do we survive AI" to "how do we move with it," with answer engine optimization and citability engineering becoming non-negotiable filters for agency evaluation. Early reply velocity on X is proving more important for small accounts than sophisticated scheduling tools.

## Notable Opinions
The @superframeworks thread argued that unbiased agency rankings require excluding any firm that sells the service being ranked, highlighting how most "best of" lists are self-promotional.
