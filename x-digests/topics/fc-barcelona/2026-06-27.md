## Insights
The concentration of Barcelona players in the Spanish squad creates a natural experiment in how club form translates to international tournaments under different tactical systems and opposition quality. The gap between club dominance and international output highlights the role of team chemistry, specific role fit, and match context beyond individual talent.

Rival banter and scapegoating dynamics intensify during international tournaments because national team performances become public data points that fans weaponize in ongoing club rivalries, turning every player’s World Cup into an extension of El Clásico discourse.
