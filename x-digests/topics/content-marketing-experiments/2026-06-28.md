# Content Marketing Experiments
**Date:** 2026-06-28

## Key Examples
@arthursbuilds launched an SEO experiment by building three free ASO tools (keyword checker, competitor checker, localization checker) as lead magnets for AppSprint, intentionally making the pages highly searchable to drive organic Google traffic and test conversion to the paid product while explicitly checking whether “Google likes me.” One client of @ani_kakapoor reached 340 profile visits per day in 30 days on Twitter/X with no cold outreach by implementing structured growth systems that free founders to focus on sales instead of manual posting. Plasma’s crypto content strategy uses a high-production debate format pitting one crypto expert against 10 traditional bankers on stablecoins, described as strong “New Media” content marketing that outperforms events and self-glazing posts. https://x.com/arthursbuilds/status/2070967624448487735 https://x.com/ani_kakapoor/status/2071120933654290730 https://x.com/GianTheRios/status/2071110220185506299

## Insights
Tactical lead-magnet pages optimized for search serve dual purposes as both acquisition tools and direct tests of platform algorithms, revealing whether organic distribution favors the creator’s positioning without relying on paid amplification. Structured Twitter growth systems shift the founder’s role from content producer to system operator, enabling consistent profile visits that compound into sales conversations while avoiding the burnout of daily manual engagement.

## Notable Opinions
More of this [high-production debate format], less events, and self glazing content.
