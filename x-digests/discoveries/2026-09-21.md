## Investigate

- **Herdr** (by @alex_verem): Open-source tool that unifies multiple AI coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, etc.) into one dashboard. Features agent state labeling (working/blocked/idle), notifications for input needs, session persistence across disconnects, and inter-agent coordination/spawning. Repo already at >39k stars. Why interesting: Practical solution for managing 5-10+ concurrent coding agents without chaos. Link: https://x.com/alex_verem/status/2101431167157461387. Suggested next step: Locate the GitHub repo (search "Herdr AI agents" or check the post thread), clone, and run a small test with 2-3 agents on a simple coding task.

- **Jev (model routing + structured output)**: Emerging tool/framework using structured output from a small/fast model to intelligently route tasks to the cheapest/suitable larger model. Discussions around real-world tests for cost savings, integration into agent pipelines, and comparisons to LLM-based routing. Why interesting: Directly addresses AI engineering pain points around cost optimization and routing without hallucinated decisions. Multiple recent tests and critiques. Links: https://x.com/morganlinton/status/2101783755648380943, https://x.com/kevinkern/status/2101722028751364311. Suggested next step: Search for "Jev AI router" or "Jev structured output" to find repo/demo, experiment with routing a few queries.

- **Multi-agent swarm orchestration patterns** (Microsoft Research / Magentic-One style): Empirical analysis showing unconstrained agent-to-agent chat in swarms wastes ~10x tokens and fails in 73% of complex tasks due to circular debates and context issues. Advocates centralized orchestrator with immutable Task Ledger and Progress Ledger for rollbacks/replanning. Why interesting: Shifts from hype to production-grade engineering patterns for reliable multi-agent systems. Strong technical signal. Link: https://x.com/damkina7/status/2101667753559994629. Suggested next step: Read the full thread or related papers on Magentic-One; prototype a simple orchestrator-ledger setup vs naive swarm.

- **AI-native creative filmmaking pipeline** (TapNow + BLACKOUT AI Studio for "Primordial"): Two creators used AI tools end-to-end (visuals, voices, performances) with human oversight on story/shot design/decisions to produce a 20-min sci-fi episode in 30 days. Featured in Variety, Venice, streaming on Glanze.tv. Why interesting: Concrete example of unconventional AI + human creative workflow compressing traditional production timelines dramatically. Link: https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2101781192856973570 (watch at https://glanze.tv/en). Suggested next step: Watch the episode and research the specific tools/workflows used in TapNow/BLACKOUT AI Studio.
