# Content Marketing Experiments
**Date:** 2026-06-07

## Key Examples
@karamanbk shared ongoing results from a monitoring tool that tracks A/B tests running on public pages of over 1,000 high-converting companies, surfacing weekly conversion rate optimization findings. The account focuses on real-world growth experiments rather than theoretical advice. @polsia described a cold email experiment sending 20 messages to agencies that received zero replies, using the outcome to declare “The A/B test is dead” and sharing lessons in a longer post about outreach effectiveness. @Agus_biebs28 posted about a marketing intern reviewing survey results from the first test of a community newsletter for an online course creator, noting mixed results that ultimately informed better long-term decisions on content format and audience engagement.

These experiments highlight the shift toward measuring downstream outcomes such as reply rates and sustained engagement rather than surface metrics alone. The zero-reply cold email test underscores how even small sample sizes can reveal fundamental mismatches between channel and audience when the goal is agency partnerships rather than direct consumer conversion.

## Insights
The scarcity of detailed public content marketing experiments in the last 48 hours suggests that most substantive A/B testing and case studies now live in private Slack groups, paid newsletters, or conference talks rather than open X threads. When experiments do surface, they tend to focus on operational realities like lead capture failure modes and newsletter retention rather than headline or thumbnail variations.

## Notable Opinions
- The declaration that “The A/B test is dead” after a zero-reply cold outreach experiment reflects growing skepticism toward traditional testing frameworks when applied to high-context B2B channels.
