# Niche Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-06-06

## Key Examples
@shapovalim, who builds AI tools specifically for service businesses, posed the question of which boring operational problems AI should quietly solve for small business owners. The list included missed calls, quote generation, follow-ups, customer notes, inbox chaos, and anything that removes monthly revenue-generating tasks from the owner's plate. The emphasis was on AI that ingests messy real-world context—mistakes, handoffs, difficult customers—and actually completes work rather than adding another chat interface. https://x.com/shapovalim/status/2063140430175404338

The "Boring Business" account highlighted how the upcoming ~$3T in AI IPOs will create a massive liquidity event for LPs, angels, and employees. This is expected to recycle capital back into new startups and heat up SF real estate as founders and early employees gain significant exits. The post framed this as an under-discussed second-order effect that will fuel an explosion of new AI ventures once liquidity hits. https://x.com/BoringBiz_/status/2063077748004802765

## Insights
Small business AI is succeeding when it focuses on removing specific, context-heavy tasks from owners rather than offering generic assistance. The distinction between "another chatbot" and "a system that knows enough context to remove a task" is becoming the key filter for what counts as useful in the boring business lane.

## Notable Opinions
@shapovalim stressed that the magic in small business AI lies in handling messy notes, handoffs, and real customer interactions that directly impact monthly revenue, rather than surface-level automation.
