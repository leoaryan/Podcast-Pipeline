# Niche Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-06-28

## Key Examples
Operators are acquiring traditional “boring” businesses through search funds and then applying AI to modernize them, with one exited $100M company founder noting that a boring business bought well and left unchanged is just a job, while returns come from modernization that AI has made 10x cheaper than two years ago. Builders report that after creating 500 AI workflows, the flashy agent builds get likes but the boring ones—automating invoicing, email triage, data entry, lead routing, call summarization, CRM updates, and document processing—get paid and deliver real ROI. One framing highlights the packaging gap: find a boring business problem, build one agent that solves it, sell to five clients for a 44% premium rather than chasing technical novelty. ## Insights
The real opportunity lies in packaging practical automation for existing paying customers in unsexy markets rather than building general agents that compete on hype, because businesses already understand their repetitive pain points and will pay for targeted time savings. This approach creates sustainable cash flow by modernizing acquired assets with low-cost AI instead of starting from zero in crowded consumer or frontier-agent spaces.

## Notable Opinions
The flashy builds get the likes. The boring ones get paid.
