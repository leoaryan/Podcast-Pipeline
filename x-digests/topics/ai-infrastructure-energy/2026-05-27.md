# AI Infrastructure & Energy
**Date:** 2026-05-27

## Key Examples
- Microsoft restarting a unit at Three Mile Island to power data centers.
- Constellation Energy securing long-term PPAs with hyperscalers.
- xAI’s Memphis supercluster as an example of rapid scaling once power is secured.

## Insights
- Power infrastructure (generation + delivery) has become the new critical path.
- Companies solving energy access are pulling ahead of those still focused only on chips.

## Notable Opinions
- The era of “just add more GPUs” is giving way to integrated energy + hardware strategy.
