# Niche Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-07-07

## Key Examples
Mike Hoffmann (@MikeHoffmann), who built a nationwide vending business while maintaining a W-2 job, shared a high-engagement thread (245 likes, 73 reposts) listing the 10 best “boring business” options for 2026. Top entries include laundromats (timeless cash-flow with automated collection), micromarkets (unattended mini convenience stores earning $15k+/month in his experience with four locations), ice machines, credit card processing terminals, vending routes (130+ machines generating ~$170k/month with minimal upkeep), self-serve car washes, storage facilities, and faceless YouTube channels outsourced on finance/health topics. He emphasizes that these unsexy models provide reliable income and reduce financial stress compared to trend-chasing ventures. https://x.com/MikeHoffmann/status/2074128862099685603

@cleanwithmike quit a $100k/year corporate job at 29 to run a remote home cleaning business that now generates $40k/month while requiring only ~5 hours of work per week. He positions the “boring” strategy as the alternative after years of chasing streaming, trading, crypto, and NFTs, stressing that chasing profits over trends delivers real results; the business is managed remotely with systems that minimize owner involvement. https://x.com/cleanwithmike/status/2074113929953849826

## Insights
Boring businesses succeed because they solve evergreen, recession-resistant needs (cleaning, storage, vending, car washes) with low customer acquisition costs and high repeatability, allowing owners to build cash-flow machines that operate with minimal daily intervention once systems are in place.

The shift from “cool” side hustles to asset-light or semi-passive physical/service businesses reflects a maturing understanding that consistent small margins on high-volume, low-drama operations compound more reliably than high-variance tech or crypto plays.
