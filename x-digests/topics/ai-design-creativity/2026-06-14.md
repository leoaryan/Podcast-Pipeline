# AI Design Creativity
**Date:** 2026-06-14

## Key Examples
@Lianaalane highlighted FIBO Edit as a significant advance in controllable AI image editing. The tool adds precision and reproducibility to generative workflows while remaining open-source, allowing users to edit specific regions with consistent outputs across runs rather than relying on stochastic prompting alone. This addresses a core limitation in earlier diffusion-based tools where small prompt changes produced unpredictable variations.

A UC Berkeley course on agentic AI design, evaluation, training, and deployment was discussed by @Twendee_. The curriculum emphasizes coordination between multiple agents, security considerations in real-world systems, and moving beyond single-model prompting to orchestrated architectures. Students are expected to build and stress-test multi-agent setups rather than just generating images or text.

## Insights
The conversation around controllable creativity tools reveals a maturing understanding that raw generative power is less valuable than predictable, editable outputs. Practitioners are prioritizing tools that support iteration and version control over one-shot generation, suggesting the next wave of creative AI will focus on human-in-the-loop refinement rather than full automation.

## Notable Opinions
@AIandDesign posted strong criticism of Anthropic’s decision to leave certain exploits unpatched, arguing it harms the broader ecosystem of creators relying on stable model behavior. This reflects growing frustration with closed providers making unilateral changes that affect downstream creative workflows.
