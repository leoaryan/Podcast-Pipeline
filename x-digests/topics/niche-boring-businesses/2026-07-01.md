# Niche Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-07-01

## Key Examples
@levikov presented a detailed case study of faceless AI character pages as an ideal “boring online business.” Revenue model: ~$34k/month net in affiliate commissions per page at 94% gross margins. Operations run with zero employees using VAs, automation, and SOPs at ~$120/month tool cost. Key attributes include predictable recurring revenue, low owner dependency (pages continue posting if the founder steps away), defensible audience (e.g., 2M followers built over 9 months), and full transferability via SOPs and logins. Exit math: one page at $408k annual profit × 3.8x multiple (average PE paid for boring online businesses in 2025) equals ~$1.55M sale price. A portfolio of six pages could exit for ~$9.3M. PE buyers focus on the P&L rather than understanding the underlying AI tech. https://x.com/levikov/status/2071987209834778708

Classic offline boring businesses such as cleaning companies, logistics, maintenance services, storage, plumbing, and local service operations continue to be highlighted for their predictable repeat demand, recurring revenue, lower competition, and durable wealth creation. One post noted that “the least exciting businesses have made more people wealthy than all the trendy ones combined.” https://x.com/guideforman/status/2071535479824998875

@BoringBiz_ shared a popular thread of 15 practical lessons for investment banking analysts, including maintaining a “master model” folder of past M&A, LBO, and spin-off models, a master slide deck for reusable templates, rigorous note-taking, rapport-building, and double-checking work. The thread embodies the boring-business ethos of systems, execution, and efficiency in a grindy but high-compensation field. https://x.com/i/status/2071411505854124034

## Insights
Faceless, automated AI pages demonstrate how modern tools can manufacture the classic attributes of boring businesses (recurring revenue, low key-man risk, transferability) at internet scale. The high margins and low capex make them attractive acquisition targets even when buyers do not deeply understand the technology, because the P&L tells a clear story of predictable cash flow.

The persistence of traditional boring businesses alongside new AI-automated variants shows that the core principles—essential demand, repeatability, and low ego involvement—remain constant even as the execution layer evolves. Builders who prioritize assets that continue generating value without constant owner attention create more durable wealth than those chasing visibility or novelty.

## Notable Opinions
Multiple replies stressed that PE multiples for boring online businesses (3.8x–8x profit observed in 2025) reward predictability and transferability over growth rate or technological novelty.
