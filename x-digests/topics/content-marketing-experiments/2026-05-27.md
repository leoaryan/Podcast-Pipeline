# Content Marketing Experiments
**Date:** 2026-05-27

## Key Examples
- Shift from 340 scattered articles to 4 focused topical maps resulting in +240% organic traffic.
- Founder building automated SEO page generation before product launch, scaling to 500+ visits/day in 5 weeks.
- 19-year-old cloning successful YouTube channel patterns and reaching $7,400+/month.

## Insights
- Depth and focus beat volume. Winners deliberately limit the number of topics they cover.
- Building the content engine early creates faster feedback loops.

## Notable Opinions
- Most people are still optimizing for publishing speed instead of authority depth.
