**Date:** 2026-06-21

## Key Examples
Investors are executing a "great rotation" out of Magnificent 7 tech stocks and parts of crypto into companies exposed to AI infrastructure bottlenecks, specifically naming T1 Energy, IREN, HIVE, and CoreWeave as targets for 2026 AI energy plays. The thesis centers on energy, power delivery, and data center capacity becoming the binding constraints as model scale and autonomous systems increase demand for reliable, high-density power.

Reliance in India emphasized AI integration combined with green hydrogen and digital infrastructure in its 2026 AGM discussions.

## Insights
The capital rotation narrative reveals that while software and model development captured early attention and valuation, the physical layer—power generation, transmission, cooling, and water management—is now emerging as the rate-limiting step, creating investment opportunities in traditionally "boring" industrial sectors that solve AI's real-world scaling problems rather than algorithmic ones.

Companies with exposure to data center water and energy needs are seeing demand signals that predate the current AI hype cycle but are now accelerating, suggesting the infrastructure buildout will have multi-year tailwinds even if model progress slows.
