# Open Source AI Tools
**Date:** 2026-06-26

## Key Examples
The standout release is Ornith-1.0, a family of open-source LLMs purpose-built for agentic coding with sizes including 9B dense, 31B/35B, and a large 397B MoE variant. It achieves strong SOTA-level results on coding and agent benchmarks such as SWE-Bench Verified, Terminal-Bench, NL2Repo, SWE Atlas, and ClawEval after training on top of Gemma 4 and Qwen 3.5 using a novel reinforcement learning approach that jointly optimizes task scaffolds and solutions rather than final outputs alone. Fully released under MIT license allowing commercial and research use, with GGUF versions available for easy local running in Ollama, Atomic Chat, Unsloth, and similar tools; the announcement generated thousands of likes and widespread testing. https://x.com/i/status/2070148887067963854

Hugging Face hosted an event titled "Welcome to Open Source AI: Run Your Own Models Locally" emphasizing accessibility and self-hosting as core to the open ecosystem. Complementary tools include policy-based LLM routers like GenLayer's "Unhardcoded" project that avoid hardcoding specific model names and instead route intelligently based on rules, cost, and performance. https://x.com/huggingface/status/2070160187751850242 https://x.com/doja_web3/status/2070395954902028633

Chinese open-weights models continue to dominate practical conversation, with a popular poll featuring Qwen 3.5, DeepSeek v4, GLM-5, and Kimi K2.6 as top options, reflecting their strong performance in the current open model landscape after surviving intense "model wars." Sentient Foundation advocates aggressively for fully open AGI with models, weights, code, data, and evals available so intelligence can be owned and built by anyone on personal or cheap hardware. https://x.com/Taniyatweets_/status/2070388259428151417 https://x.com/sentient_found/status/2070397278645559739

## Insights
The scaffold-optimization technique in Ornith-1.0—jointly training on task scaffolds alongside solutions—represents a shift from outcome-only optimization to process-aware training, which likely explains its strong agentic coding results and suggests that future open models will increasingly incorporate explicit workflow or tool-use structures during pre-training or RL stages rather than relying solely on prompting.

The dominance of Chinese labs in open-weights performance combined with Western efforts like Sentient Foundation's push for open AGI creates a geopolitical dynamic where open source becomes a battleground for accessibility versus control; builders gain immediate practical benefits from local, free, high-performing models while the broader movement pressures closed providers to justify their restrictions.

## Notable Opinions
Community reaction to Ornith-1.0 centers on "open-source must win," with users actively testing locally and requesting additional sizes around 120B while praising the scaffold approach as a meaningful technical advance. https://x.com/AIwithAliya/status/2070167034739261737
