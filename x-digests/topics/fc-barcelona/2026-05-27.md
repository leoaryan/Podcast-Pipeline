# FC Barcelona
**Date:** 2026-05-27

## Key Examples
- Burnley (19th in Premier League) earned more from TV revenue than Barcelona from winning La Liga.
- Current focus on João Pedro and Julián Álvarez, with openness to permanently selling Ansu Fati.
- Spain’s national team included 8 Barcelona players and 0 from Real Madrid.

## Insights
- Barcelona’s squad-building is heavily shaped by severe financial constraints compared to mid-table Premier League clubs.
- Reliance on internal development and targeted, lower-cost moves.

## Notable Opinions
- Barcelona must be smarter rather than bigger spenders.
