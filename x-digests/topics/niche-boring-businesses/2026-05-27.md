# Niche & Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-05-27

## Key Examples
- @AlfieJCarter broke down 31 Claude skills for small business workflows (finance, sales, HR, marketing).
- @polsia building narrow agents for local service businesses (ResponseIQ, CapCall, FieldFlow).
- Arizona business owner using Etchie agent for supply chain and refunds while offline.

## Insights
- Highest-leverage automations for local businesses are fast lead response and appointment booking.
- Many implementations start with 4–7 well-wired skills rather than trying all 31.

## Notable Opinions
- Implementation and trust-building matter as much as the tools themselves.
