# Niche Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Key Examples
Boring businesses like HVAC, plumbing, clinics, and laundromats with stable cash flow and no succession plans (1M+ profitable Main Street companies, 85% boomer-owned) are being positioned as an AI hedge because physical workflows resist full disruption. Hermes Agent is used to deliver automation services charging $500–2,000/month recurring per client for lead gen, content, customer service, and admin, with dedicated agents per client that hunt gigs, draft pitches, seek approval via Telegram, and compound skills over time. @BoringBiz_ emphasizes AI as an enhancement tool that builds on pre-AI fundamentals rather than replacement, noting that engineers who coded before ChatGPT outperform vibe coders in real execution. https://x.com/BoringBiz_/status/2062663084196667848

One setup involves personal CFO agents that find overcharges, job hunter agents, knowledge base builders, and infra monitoring running locally on self-hosted hardware via Ollama to avoid cloud subscriptions and privacy issues. Multiple clients can be served simultaneously because the agent handles most of the work after initial setup, creating recurring revenue while the operator focuses on sales and oversight. @zeuuss_01 and @max_paperclips shared specific Hermes configurations for these use cases. https://x.com/zeuuss_01/status/2062359687664673147

## Insights
The meta-play of selling persistent local automation services to traditional boring businesses generates more discussion than actually acquiring the businesses at scale, because execution requires daily operator involvement in the trenches rather than passive ownership.

Real alpha comes from rebuilding marketing and operations fundamentals that many acquired businesses lack, using agents to handle consistent testing and content that boomer operators often neglect, but this still demands human judgment on what to automate versus what stays manual.

## Notable Opinions
@knowerofmarkets noted the irony that LinkedIn hype around AI-powered roll-ups of boring businesses far outpaces the number of people willing to operate the tools daily, highlighting that sustainable value requires choosing the right targets rather than chasing full autonomy.
