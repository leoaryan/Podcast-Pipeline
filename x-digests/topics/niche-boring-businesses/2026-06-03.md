# Niche & Boring Businesses
**Date:** 2026-06-03

## Key Examples
- **Home service job leads SaaS: $68.6K ARR, asking $100K on @acquiredotcom** — A real-time job leads platform for HVAC, plumbing, and pool service pros operating in 4 states with no pay-per-lead model. This is the textbook "niche SaaS serving boring businesses" play: unsexy, high retention, real operational pain. The platform generates revenue through subscriptions rather than per-lead fees, giving service pros predictable costs. Asking price of $100K for $68.6K ARR implies roughly 1.5x multiple — attractive for individual acquirers. Source: x.com/acquiredotcom/status/2061473858117394752
- **Healthcare scheduling SaaS: $175K TTM revenue, asking $424K on @acquiredotcom** — Web-based healthcare appointment scheduling and referrals. The 2.4x multiple reflects the stickiness of healthcare workflows — once integrated into a practice's daily operations, switching costs are extremely high. This is the kind of "boring" business that generates steady cash flow with minimal customer churn. Source: x.com/acquiredotcom/status/2061927594010796073
- **AI-powered crafting SaaS: $24.6K TTM, 96% growth, asking $59.8K on @microacquire** — 5,000+ users, 68K+ assets. Demonstrates that even "boring" can have an AI angle — the growth rate suggests strong product-market fit in a niche creative community. The low multiple (2.4x) on high growth suggests the acquirer community still underweights AI-enhanced niche products. Source: x.com/microacquire/status/2061251641773285785

## Insights
- acquire.com deal flow is robust in mid-2026. Multiple listings per week in the $50K-$500K range with clean financials and recurring revenue. The platform has become the de facto marketplace for small SaaS acquisitions.
- The "boring business" thesis continues to hold: these companies have sticky customers, predictable revenue, and lower competition than consumer apps. The retirement wave among traditional service business owners creates acquisition opportunities on both the operating company and software sides.
- AI is filtering into even the most "boring" niches — but the winning approach is embedding AI as a feature within an existing workflow (scheduling, lead gen, compliance) rather than building an "AI for X" company from scratch.

## Notable Opinions
- @TrentBizBroker: "Boring businesses like HVAC, plumbing, and pool services are often the most resilient and hard to replace." Source: x.com/TrentBizBroker/status/2061824527781302758
- Multiple founders note: the 2026 environment favors operators who understand small business pain points over pure technologists.
