# AI Infrastructure & Energy
**Date:** 2026-07-05

## Key Examples
Joulent raised $1.75B for AI energy infrastructure in the largest U.S. round of the week, with other deals including $800M into Together AI (led by Aramco Ventures at $8.3B valuation) and $1.5B into Baseten. Sovereign wealth funds and strategic corporate investors are now the marginal buyers, prioritizing strategic positioning and energy/security needs over pure IRR/10x returns, which changes how valuations are interpreted in this sector. https://x.com/ebrahimzadem/status/2073657856054718506

AI infrastructure is entering a “trust era” where the ability to secure reliable energy matters more than speed of deployment. Recent regulatory and policy signals from Singapore, Australia, and South Korea highlight energy access as the new competitive moat, with grid strain intensifying and companies like Alphabet prioritizing self-generation and dedicated power solutions. https://x.com/Yingfluencer01/status/2073651669687238677

Projects are tokenizing physical AI layers (GPUs, data centers, and energy infrastructure) as real-world assets (RWAs) that are verified, insured, and yield-bearing onchain. One example pairs execution layers with tokenized energy/GPU capacity for AI agents, creating new financial instruments around compute and power. https://x.com/Biggest_SLy27/status/2073654246424080590

Traders highlight public market plays tied to the theme, including energy storage and grid-scale batteries ($EOSE), data centers combining Bitcoin mining + AI compute ($CIFR), advanced batteries ($AMPX), solar manufacturing ($TE), GPU/cloud AI data centers ($WYFI), and compound semiconductors/photonics ($AXTI). https://x.com/Antniosang/status/2073629159138439558

## Insights
Energy has shifted from a background concern to the central constraint for AI scaling, driving capital flows into dedicated power solutions and forcing strategic investors to distort traditional valuation signals in favor of long-term positioning. This creates a non-obvious dynamic where sovereign funds become key players, changing the risk/return profile of AI infrastructure investments away from pure venture-style multiples.

The intersection of tokenized RWAs with energy and compute infrastructure opens pathways for decentralized financing of AI buildout, where yield-bearing assets tied to physical power and GPUs could democratize access but also introduce new volatility and regulatory complexities not present in pure software AI plays.

## Notable Opinions
The ability to secure reliable energy is becoming a greater moat than deployment speed, with policy signals from multiple countries reinforcing energy access as the decisive factor in AI infrastructure competition.
